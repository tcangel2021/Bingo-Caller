📘 Teacher Classroom Guide & ViewBoard Shortcuts

1. Physical Card Mode (Default for Classroom Printouts):
Students hold printed Bingo cards at their desks. Click RANDOM DRAW (or press Spacebar) to announce one word at a time on your ViewBoard. Every word is automatically logged with its draw number and timestamp so duplicates never happen.
2. Verifying a Student's "BINGO!" Call:
Click BINGO! VERIFY (or press V). The timer pauses automatically and opens a searchable checklist of all drawn words so you can confirm the student's physical card before celebrating with confetti!
3. Saving & Sharing Lesson Lists (JSON):
Use Export JSON to save your custom vocabulary list to your USB drive or Google Drive, and Import JSON next semester to load it in seconds. All progress also auto-saves in your browser.
Keyboard Shortcuts (Desktop / Wireless Presenter Clicker):
  • Spacebar or Right Arrow (→): Draw Next Item
  • Left Arrow (←): View Previous Drawn Item
  • P: Pause / Resume Timer
  • V: Open Bingo Verification Panel
  • F: Toggle Fullscreen ViewBoard Mode
